🐝 Bee Dash Game Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026 | Last Updated: July 25, 2026

Welcome to Bee Dash: The Great Honey Rescue ("Bee Dash", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Kuan Meng See.

We place the highest priority on protecting the privacy of our players—especially children and families. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you install and play Bee Dash on iOS and Android devices.

1. Summary (No Data Collected)

Bee Dash is a 100% privacy-friendly game.

  • We do NOT collect, request, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • We do NOT display third-party advertisements.
  • We do NOT use tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, or advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or GAID).
  • We do NOT require an account, email address, or registration to play.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide game progression features, Bee Dash saves non-personal game settings and score data directly on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences).

This local data includes:

  • Game progress (completed levels, unlocked chapters, high scores, collected honey coins)
  • Unlocked cosmetic items (bee skins, trails, and power-up inventory)
  • App preferences (sound/music toggles, language preference, and Zen mode toggles)

Important: All local data remains exclusively on your physical device. It is never uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Bee Dash is designed to be enjoyable and safe for players of all ages, including children.

We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Apple's / Google's Kids Category policies:

  • We do not collect any personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • The game contains no external links, social media integration, or unprotected web browsers.
  • The game contains no real-money In-App Purchases (IAP).

4. Third-Party Services & Links

Bee Dash operates entirely standalone and offline. It does not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs) for behavioral tracking, advertising networks, or social logins.

5. Data Deletion & Control

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • Reset In-Game Progress: You can clear your save data at any time from the in-game Settings menu.
  • Delete All Data: Uninstalling the Bee Dash app from your device will permanently remove all locally saved data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal compliance requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
